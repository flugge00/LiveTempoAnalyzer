I want to create a Live Tempo Analyzer.
The problem that it is trying to solve is that it is hard when rehersing or playing live (without clicktrack) to know if you play in time or if you shift along the way. Sometimes you might not end up at the same tempo as you started or that sometimes the verse goes slower while the chorus goes faster. 
I want a tool to help me practise and understand our behaviour when we are rehersing live. 
A tool to help us become better musicians and play better as a group.

I want a live tempo analyzer, which uses the microphone of my device, tablet or phone, but also desktop possibility. 
It should listen to the audio from the live microphone and extract the tempo in realtime. I want to plot this tempo in a moving graph so I can see if we are shifting, and especially if we are shifting over time. This is more meant to understand if we are drifting the tempo rather then shifting tempo within a bar. 
I also want the possiblity to inspect this graph afterwards to see and understand more facts about it. Then maybe have some stats about tightness within the band, and tempo inside bars and stuff.
