Working…

    Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-click to fit. Click to play from that point. Drag the handles on the coloured strip to move a section boundary.

    Song sections

    Found automatically from repeating harmony and sound. Names are guesses: type over a name to fix it. To split a section, click the chart where the new part starts. To join one with the next, click its row.

    Timing inside the bar

    Average offset of each beat against an even grid through its bar. Negative is early (rushing).

    How to read these numbers
    Tempo curve
    Tempo measured over a sliding 8-second window. Smooth, and good for spotting drift over minutes.
    Per-bar tempo
    Tempo from the length of each single bar, from one detected "1" to the next. It jumps much more than the 8-second line, and that's mostly real: at about 125 BPM a bar is under 2 seconds, so when each beat of a bar comes about 20 ms early, that bar reads about 5 BPM faster. Most bars stay within ±1 BPM of the line. The big jumps usually sit in fills, pushes into a chorus, or a slowing ending.
    Bars and the "1"
    With Bar starts on beat: Auto, the first beat of each bar is found from chord changes, the kick drum and the snare backbeat. Press Play and watch the beat counter next to the time. If the "1" is off, choose the right beat by hand.
    Drift
    End tempo minus start tempo (medians of the first and last 15 seconds).
    Steadiness (±)
    Standard deviation of the tempo. Lower is steadier.
    Timing jitter
    How far individual beats land from a smooth local grid (RMS, ms). Around 5–10 ms is tight; above 20 ms is loose.
    Attack spread
    How smeared each beat's attack is (ms). When everyone hits together the attack is one sharp spike; flams and loose playing spread it out. It's measured from one mixed recording, so treat it as a trend indicator rather than an exact number.
